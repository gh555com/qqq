<p align="left">

Press `Space` on selected item: ignore any preferences, request its size.

Press `1` to scroll to list top.
Press `2` to scroll to list bottom.


  Make the most of the **`Q`** key to open projects or documents;  make the most of the **`W`** key to open your music, videos, and games.
